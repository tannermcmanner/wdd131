Milestones

Over the years, the sport of parkour has been pushed to higher and higher limits, resulting in more and more seemingly impossible tricks being done. In addition, the growth of parkour has also seeped into mainstream media. This page will cover a few notable milestones in the sport.

Parkour in The Office

In the 2007 episode "Hot Girl," Michael Scott and his coworkers attempt parkour around the office. The scene was a lighthearted joke, but it showed how recognizable parkour had become and introduced the sport to millions of mainstream viewers.

Devin Super Tramp's Assassin's Creed Videos

Devin Graham, known as Devin Super Tramp, filmed live-action parkour videos inspired by Assassin's Creed. Their cinematic quality and huge view counts showed that parkour could look as epic as a big-budget game, and they brought the sport to a new generation of fans.

Tracers

Tracers, starring Taylor Lautner, was a Hollywood action film built around parkour. It brought the sport to the big screen and gave practitioners a major feature film that centered on their world.

Storror's Roof Culture Asia

Roof Culture Asia is a documentary by the British parkour team Storror, following them across Asia as they train on rooftops and landmarks. Its scale and production value set a new standard for parkour documentaries, and showed how far the sport had grown.

The Standing Double Backflip

Nick Fry performed the world's first recorded standing double backflip on flat, solid ground, a trick long thought to be nearly impossible without a trampoline or soft surface. It is so advanced that only 16 people worldwide have documented themselves doing it.

The 360 Dive Kong Gainer Full

Calen Chan completed the world's first 360 dive kong gainer full, combining a dive kong with a backward-traveling gainer and a full twist. It pushed the limits of what a single obstacle could be used for and showed how much creativity remains in the sport.

Lyon 25

Dom Tomato was the first person ever to front flip down the Lyon 25, a staircase well known as a skateboarding location. The impact badly bruised his heel, but the feat showed how far athletes will go to push the limits of the sport.
