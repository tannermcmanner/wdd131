Site Name

Parkourpedia

The name combines "parkour" with the suffix "-pedia," signaling an encyclopedia-style resource.

Optional domain availability: parkourpedia.com

Site Purpose

The site's purpose is to inform users on the history and some major milestones of the sport.

Scenarios

Scenario questions a site visitor might ask:

  1. "Where did parkour actually come from, and who started it?"
  2. "What are some of the biggest milestones in parkour history?""

Color Schema

The palette aims to reflect the feel of parkour's urban training style.

Typography

Two fonts, loaded from Google Fonts, will be used across the site:

Permanent Marker

Used for the logo, page titles, and section headings. Permanent Marker mimics bold spray-paint lettering, matching parkour's urban street culture.

Open Sans

Used for body text, navigation, and captions. Keeps text easier to read.
